Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

JasD DataHub is a free browser-based tool created by James Wambugu (JasD Digital Solutions).

Your data

Records, imported files and reports are processed in your browser and saved in your browser's local storage. They are not uploaded to our servers. Clearing your browser data removes them. If you choose the optional cloud feature, data is sent to the Firebase project you configure yourself.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics (with IP anonymisation) to count visits and button clicks, such as exports and contact links. This does not include the contents of your records.

Advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com.

Third-party services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and, when you use import features, document-processing libraries from public CDNs.
