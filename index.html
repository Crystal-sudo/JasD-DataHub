DataHub Information

Data Privacy

JasD DataHub is designed with a local-first workflow. Records and datasets handled by the core browser tools are processed in the browser unless an optional cloud feature is deliberately connected by the user.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate website usage, such as pages or features visited and general interaction patterns. Analytics data is separate from the browser-local records workspace.

Uploaded files

Do not upload confidential, regulated or personal information unless you are authorized to process it and understand the environment in which you are working.

Terms of Use

JasD DataHub is provided as a practical data-management and demonstration workspace. Users remain responsible for the accuracy, legality and authorization of the data they process.

  • Review automated suggestions before relying on them.
  • Keep appropriate backups of important source data.
  • Do not use the workspace to bypass organizational privacy or security controls.
  • Optional cloud integrations should use the user's own properly secured project and rules.

About JasD DataHub

JasD DataHub is a free, browser-based workspace for data entry, records management, data cleansing and validation. It is built by James Wambugu of JasD Digital Solutions for students, data-entry clerks, analysts and teams who want to practise or perform data-quality work without installing software.

What you can do

  • Add, edit and search records, then export them as CSV.
  • Import CSV and Excel data and find missing values, duplicates and formatting errors.
  • Work through flawed-dataset challenges and get a score against a rubric.
  • Keep an audit trail of every correction and export an evaluation report.

How it works

  1. Import a file or add records manually.
  2. Review the issues DataHub detects and correct them.
  3. Check your score and export the report or cleaned data.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free? Yes, with no signup.

Is my data uploaded? No. Data stays in your browser's local storage. Optional cloud sync uses your own Firebase project.

Who built it? James Wambugu, an IT professional in Kenya – see JasD Digital Solutions.
